## Changed files and dependency policy

All new work is confined to `experiments/grok_swarm_lab/`. New source is under
`lggr_swarm_lab/`, `tests/`, and evidence/reproduction helpers. `protocol.json`,
README, report, traces, results, source manifests and checksums describe this
experiment. No upstream runtime, test, manuscript, or Lean source was patched.
Consequently there is no dependency-correction patch: the host's narrower
interfaces are the correction strategy. The source bundle includes the exact
baseline dependency and documents its known defects rather than silently
replacing it with repaired code.
