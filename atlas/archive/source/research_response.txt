## A. Mathematical answer

The useful contribution is a criterion for what a local certificate retains, plus conditions for transporting and composing that evidence. It is not a general theorem that self-modifying systems drift, and it does not outperform a complete fixed-invariant verifier on the example below.

If every accepted transition preserves the same predicate \(I\), then \(I(S_0)\) and \(\forall i,\ I(S_i)\Rightarrow I(S_{i+1})\) imply \(I(S_n)\) by induction. Reading the evaluator from accepted main helps protect this premise from candidate tampering; it does not establish that the evaluator proves it. A test suite can remain immutable while its coverage is incomplete. Likewise, a bound on each incremental change need not bound their cumulative effect.

**The strongest directly applicable result is** \( \texttt{WitnessedDifference.ComponentReports.decoder_xor_collision}\), at lines 94–101 of the WDP paper's ComponentReports.lean [E1]. For arbitrary types \(X,A,B\), declare a report \(p:X\to A\), a protected query \(q:X\to B\), and the realized report image \(p(X)\). Exactly one of these holds:

\[
\exists D:p(X)\to B,\quad D(p(x))=q(x)\ \text{for every }x;
\qquad
\exists x,y,\quad p(x)=p(y),\ q(x)\ne q(y).
\]

The proof establishes decoder existence iff fiber constancy; failure gives a collision. A decoder is unique on the realized image. There is no finiteness, linearity, topology, quantum structure, or independent-model assumption. This is standard factorization mathematics, packaged with explicit countermodels and related gluing results. I independently checked the original source with Lean 4.35.0; the audited declaration uses only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. Its classical existential proof is not an algorithm for finding a decoder or collision in an arbitrary infinite system.

For this application, let \(X\) be complete modification histories in a declared model, \(p\) the retained evaluation reports, and \(q\) the global requirement. A collision is a checkable certificate that those reports cannot determine the requirement. It does **not** identify which history occurred, unless history evidence is also supplied. It proves insufficiency of the report, not failure of every state producing that report. When one recorded history is independently shown to violate the requirement, the violation and information deficit become two separate certified facts.

LGGR I supplies the corresponding evaluator result, PaperAE004.verdict_ceiling: every evaluator depending only on a colliding report must disagree with the Boolean target on at least one member of the pair [E2]. Further processing of the same report cannot separate that pair. Additional models therefore help only insofar as they add relevant distinctions, or improve use of information already retained. This is a deterministic statement about shared information, not a statistical claim about model independence. An injective source representation has no such information-loss obstruction; reasoning about it may still be computationally difficult. We must not pretend that a reviewer seeing full code receives only pass bits.

A transition witness must say what it witnesses. In the experiment it contains endpoints, evaluator version, observed change, and the local acceptance result. That verifies a bounded step, not a bounded history. A mathematical composition witness instead connects those endpoint facts to the requested global proposition. Hoare-style certificates compose when the first postcondition entails the second precondition and both interpretations refer to the same protected semantics. Across representations, this requires explicit interpretation maps and commuting compatibility equations. WDP's composed_square proves how supplied commuting squares paste [E6]; it does not derive their premises from successful tests.

There are two distinct notions of descent. A query descends through a report when it is fiber-constant. Local functions descend across a cover when their restrictions agree on overlaps: ComponentReports.glue_exists_unique constructs the unique function on the union under exactly that hypothesis [E1]. Arbitrary presheaves need not satisfy existence or uniqueness; the same module contains finite countermodels. Moreover, gluing compatible data does not establish that the resulting function satisfies a separate safety constraint. In our budget example the endpoint data compose perfectly; the composed value is simply outside the permitted bound.

LGGR I's kernel-composition theorem assumes pointwise factorization of the evaluated contribution, as well as the declared path decomposition [E3]. This assumption is substantive: it cannot be cited as a derivation of global acceptance from local acceptance. The Boolean defect \(\Delta_E=E_g-E_1E_2\) records disagreement once \(E_g\) has been obtained. Computing that defect does not supply the missing global evaluator.

The linear obstruction machinery applies only after specifying an actual repair problem. Given a rational matrix \(D\), defect \(h\), and positive weights, define \(\Phi(h)=\min_x\sum_e w_e|h_e-(Dx)_e|\), where attainment must be justified or certified. A dual vector \(z\) satisfying \(D^\mathsf{T}z=0\) and \(|z_e|\le w_e\) gives a lower bound \(z^\mathsf{T}h\le\Phi(h)\); a matching primal residual certifies optimality [E5]. This can produce a reusable finite incompatibility certificate. It is not merely a failed search.

Cohomology requires more: specified differentials with \(d_1d_0=0\), a closed cochain, and quotienting by exact repairs. A nonclosed cochain has a closure failure, not an \(H^1\) class. A closed cochain may still be nonexact. Conversely, the cumulative-budget counterexample has exact endpoint differences and zero cycle defect. Calling its violation “nonzero cohomology” would be wrong. The simplest adequate checker is arithmetic on a retained baseline.

A representation change preserves repair equivalence when its maps commute with the repair operator. Preserving **nonrepairability** needs an additional reflection property: the WDP retraction_reflects_repair theorem supplies one, while Finite-QM's ChainRetraction.Hmap_injective proves the analogous homology result for linear chain retractions [E6, E12]. A lossy map can erase a visible obstruction. A zero target residual then says nothing about repair of the original source unless the relevant query or class is faithfully reflected. The Finite-QM growth theorem likewise needs compatible restrictions, extending cochains and nondecreased weights [E18].

The two engineering witnesses establish different propositions: the old evaluator reports acceptance; the running system reports behavior on the smoke-test observations. Both can be correct while a longer-history property fails. The missing evidence may be a baseline comparison, an interface-compatibility proof, a transport certificate, or an explicitly unresolved obligation. Neither independent identities nor agreement alone proves completeness of their combined observations. The authority model separates residuals from grants by type; it does not prove grantor legitimacy or prevent correlated reasoning errors [E14, E16].

Finite quantum work contributes useful examples of why compatible observations need not extend to an admitted global model, particularly when positivity is required. It does not establish equivalence between quantum contextuality and software drift. The observer-presheaf paper itself distinguishes no-signalling, global support, positive extension and cover-relative gluing failure [E10]. The freshly checked Finite-QM parity theorem proves nonexistence for one declared finite constraint system, not an AI no-go theorem [E11]. Hilbert, scalar and transition-probability laws retain explicit admission boundaries [E22].

The practical proposal is therefore modest: retain the proposition, representation, source identity, scope, and unresolved obligations with each witness; demand a composition or transport check before reusing it for a larger claim. Compared with a complete immutable reference verifier, no stronger detection guarantee has been demonstrated. Possible advantages are smaller replayable certificates, better localization of missing information, and fewer silently lost obligations. These are testable engineering benefits, presently **PROPOSED**, rather than established superiority. An equal-information comparison against ordinary regression and contract checking could falsify them.

## B. Minimal worked example and exact experiment

This is a finite model of incomplete verification, not a diagnosis of the engineer's implementation. The full available source might already make the missing check easy.

Let the configuration value be \(x\in\{0,1,2\}\). Keep the evaluator code, specification, and probes fixed in accepted main. Its implemented rule is
\[
A(x,y)\iff x,y\in\{0,1,2\}\ \land\ |y-x|\le1.
\]
The human-declared cumulative requirement is \(G(x_0,\ldots,x_n)\iff |x_n-x_0|\le1\). The local gate has not proved that requirement. Workers change only the configuration. Smoke tests check the legal configuration domain. Protected adversarial probes continue to reject the direct jump \(0\to2\) and the out-of-domain jump \(0\to3\).

| Transition | Previous evaluator's observations | Local witness and decision | Running-system observation | Cumulative requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline \(S_0=0\) | Legal domain, baseline recorded | Initial accepted state | Legal domain | \(0\le1\) |
| \(0\to1\) | Domain valid; change magnitude 1 | Endpoints and rule version prove \(A(0,1)\) | Domain valid | \(1\le1\), passes |
| \(1\to2\) | Domain valid; change magnitude 1 | Endpoints and rule version prove \(A(1,2)\) | Domain valid | \(2\le1\), fails |

Nothing edits or bypasses a protected test. Both local witnesses are true. They cannot prove \(A(0,2)\), which is false. If an actual protected gate already verifies \(G\) after every change, it rejects the second step and this example does not apply.

The good history \((0,1,0)\) has exactly the same sequence of observed magnitudes, local verdicts and smoke verdicts as the bad history \((0,1,2)\), but opposite global verdicts. For **that restricted report**, these histories constitute the decoder theorem's collision. Retained full endpoints distinguish them; adding the signed increments \(+1,+1\) is one sufficient refinement. A cryptographic digest used as a unique identifier is not mathematically identical to a pass-bit report, and this example makes no hash-collision claim.

| Method | Result on the second step | Required information |
|---|---|---|
| A: previous-state acceptance and the stated smoke tests | Accepts | Previous and proposed states; local rule |
| B: fixed-baseline/end-to-end check | Rejects | Baseline 0, final state 2, global bound 1 |
| C: proposed composition adapter, with signed endpoint witnesses | Rejects; emits proposed OBSTRUCTION_DETECTED | The same baseline, signed changes and bound |
| C with only the two local pass reports | WITNESS_INCOMPLETE | It cannot honestly infer the realized global result |

There is **no additional detection capability over B** in this example. C makes the missing proof obligation and the lossy report explicit.

The adapter outcome names are proposed for this demonstration. The existing EPK names are DefectKind.NGD, ZDR and PGD [E19]. The executed bad trace has \((E_1,E_2,E_g)=(1,1,0)\), hence \(\Delta_E=-1\) and NGD. Existing WDP routing also has separate NoDescentYet, SearchIncomplete, PolicyUnderspecified and other terminals [E13]. These are not interchangeable with proved violation. AUTHORITY_REQUIRED in the demonstration flags a changed authority regime; the program does not verify a real grant.

A separate linear compatibility example shows what a genuine repair certificate looks like:
\[
D=\begin{pmatrix}-1&1&0\\0&-1&1\\-1&0&1\end{pmatrix},
\quad h=(1,1,1),\quad z=(1,1,-1).
\]
Here \(D^\mathsf{T}z=0\), \(\|z\|_\infty=1\), and \(z^\mathsf{T}h=1\). Thus every unit-weight L1 repair has residual at least 1. The potential \((0,1,2)\) has residual \((0,0,-1)\), attaining 1. This certifies \(\Phi=1\), rather than merely failing to find a repair. On an unfilled triangle it is a nonexact 1-cochain with vacuous degree-two closure; on the filled triangle its circulation is nonzero, so it is not an \(H^1\) class. It is a separate incompatibility model, not a claimed encoding of the budget violation. The budget history's differences \((1,1,2)\) are exact.

**Fresh execution:** the pinned EPK implementation returned NGD; all 8 Boolean verdict triples and all 216 combinations of a three-state/three-report map and Boolean query passed the stated checks. The exact linear certificate above passed. The existing section_i_census returned its colliding pair, negative defect −1 and pass-count gap 1. These finite checks support the examples; they do not replace the general Lean proof.

It reads one source file from the pinned local Git object database, imports NumPy through the existing environment, and writes no repository files. ```python
```python
import hashlib, itertools, json, subprocess, sys, types
REPO = r"C:/Users/a343o/source/repos/the_symbol_game"
PIN = "e7420ae75737df9dbb0785cc102e8179fa6feb8c"
PATH = ("omega_engine_atlas/coda_atlas/F004_papers/"
        "001_finite_obstruction_calculus/"
        "001_local_green_global_red_foundational_series/"
        "lggr_i_evaluated_path_kernels/code/epk_core.py")
raw = subprocess.check_output(["git", "-C", REPO, "show", PIN + ":" + PATH])
epk = types.ModuleType("pinned_epk")
sys.modules[epk.__name__] = epk
exec(compile(raw, PATH, "exec"), epk.__dict__)

states = (0, 1, 2)
def accepted(old, new):
    return old in states and new in states and abs(new - old) <= 1

def smoke(state):
    return state in states

def target(history):
    return abs(history[-1] - history[0]) <= 1

def reports(history):
    return tuple((abs(b-a), accepted(a, b), smoke(b))
                 for a, b in zip(history, history[1:]))

def audit(history, retain_signed_witness=True, authority_changed=False):
    if authority_changed:
        return "AUTHORITY_REQUIRED"
    if not retain_signed_witness:
        return "WITNESS_INCOMPLETE"
    total = sum(b-a for a, b in zip(history, history[1:]))
    assert total == history[-1]-history[0]
    return "COMPOSITION_VERIFIED" if abs(total) <= 1 else "OBSTRUCTION_DETECTED"

bad, good = (0, 1, 2), (0, 1, 0)
assert all(accepted(a,b) and smoke(b) for a,b in zip(bad,bad[1:]))
assert not accepted(0,2) and not accepted(0,3)  # protected negative probes
assert reports(bad) == reports(good)
assert not target(bad) and target(good)
triple = epk.EvaluatorVerdictTriple(1, 1, int(target(bad)))
assert triple.delta_eval == -1 and triple.defect_kind is epk.DefectKind.NGD

verdict_cases = 0
for a,b,g in itertools.product((0,1), repeat=3):
    t = epk.EvaluatorVerdictTriple(a,b,g)
    assert t.delta_eval == g-a*b
    assert epk.calculate_evaluator_defect(a,b,g) == g-a*b
    verdict_cases += 1

# Exhaustively test the report/query factorization criterion on a 3-state carrier.
factor_cases = 0
for report in itertools.product(range(3), repeat=3):
    for query in itertools.product((0,1), repeat=3):
        constant = all(report[x] != report[y] or query[x] == query[y]
                       for x,y in itertools.product(range(3),repeat=2))
        decodes = any(all(d[report[x]] == query[x] for x in range(3))
                      for d in itertools.product((0,1),repeat=3))
        assert constant == decodes
        factor_cases += 1

# A separate exact cochain certificate; not an encoding of the budget violation.
D0 = ((-1,1,0),(0,-1,1),(-1,0,1))
h, z = (1,1,1), (1,1,-1)
assert all(sum(z[e]*D0[e][v] for e in range(3)) == 0 for v in range(3))
assert sum(z[e]*h[e] for e in range(3)) == 1
assert max(abs(v) for v in z) == 1
potential = (0,1,2)
residual = tuple(h[e]-sum(D0[e][v]*potential[v] for v in range(3))
                 for e in range(3))
assert sum(map(abs,residual)) == 1  # matches dual lower bound, so Phi=1
print(json.dumps({
 "authority_effect":"None", "source_commit":PIN,
 "epk_sha256":hashlib.sha256(raw).hexdigest(),
 "bad_history":bad, "good_history":good, "same_reports":reports(bad),
 "local_verdicts":[1,1], "global_verdict":0,
 "existing_defect_kind":triple.defect_kind.name, "delta":triple.delta_eval,
 "proposed_outcomes":[audit(bad),audit(good),audit(bad,False),audit(bad,True,True)],
 "all_eight_boolean_triples":verdict_cases, "finite_factorization_cases":factor_cases,
 "cochain_example":{"h":h,"dual":z,"repair":potential,"residual":residual,"Phi":1},
 "limitation":"Proposed audit labels are adapter vocabulary; no general AI safety or cohomology claim."
},indent=2))

```

## Adversarial review and smallest remaining experiment
