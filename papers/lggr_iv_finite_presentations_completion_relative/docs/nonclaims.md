# Non-Claims and Scope Boundaries — LGGR IV

To maintain strict claim hygiene, the results of this paper are bounded by the following explicit non-claims:

1. **Diagnostic Geometry, Not Physics or Spacetime**:
   The divergence between $\mathbb{Z}$ and its $2$-adic completion $\mathbb{Z}_2$ is a deterministic mathematical property of projective systems and metric completions. It does not model physical spacetime quantization, quantum foam, vacuum energy, or quantum measurement collapse.

2. **Thread Existence vs. Original-Carrier Descent**:
   The nonemptiness theorem for inverse systems of finite nonempty sets (Stacks Tag 086J) establishes that a coherent thread exists in the inverse limit (completion), precluding failure of thread existence. However, it does not ensure that the thread descends to an element of the original carrier $X$. In abelian towers, Mittag–Leffler kernel transitions (Stacks Tag 07KV) preclude lifting failure of compatible families ($R^1 \varprojlim A_n = 0$). Those two classical statements are citations. The proved rows of this deposit are the finite-chain thread and the finite overlap identity.

3. **Representation Limits vs. Reality**:
   Moving-witness collisions under $\neg\operatorname{FinDesc}(D)$ reflect the unavoidable lossiness of finite observation projections $\pi_i$, not an ontological defect in the underlying state space.

4. **No Continuous Extrapolation**:
   Verifying a countable tower of discrete congruence tests provides formal evidence only for those tests. It does not authorize unverified assertions regarding continuum dynamics.
