Task-relative recovery and bounded recursive improvement
2026-10-23

## Abstract

Agents, records, instruments and evaluators are parts of the world they describe. Within an explicitly declared model, we characterize what a record determines, which tasks a memory can recover, and which distinctions an updater must preserve for future use. Exact recovery is equivalent to constancy on observation fibers when representatives are supplied. Future equivalence is the greatest update-stable relation preserving the declared readout. Constructive Lean proofs establish factivity, positive introspection, knowledge of one's own record, and preservation under every finite action sequence. Executable checked append and recall operations reject conflicting data, and a guarded transition preserves a declared invariant through arbitrary finite executions. These positive results accompany the earlier finite-carrier obstruction: indefinitely many distinct improvements cannot be represented by a fixed finite carrier. That result does not limit expanding histories or all forms of learning. Exact finite experiments exhibit a memory enlargement from one to two bits when a new action is admitted, a sharp recovery-error witness, and the advantage of adaptive observation. The companion paper supplies quantitative recovery, experiment-simulation and query-budget formalizations. Every result identifies its observation model, task and resource assumptions; no viewpoint outside the world is required. A source-checked persistent memory now enters the repository agent context before dispatch. Separate-process live trials demonstrate exact reuse of a learned observation and retain an arithmetic failure after correct recall. These finite results distinguish reliable memory access from reliable reasoning.

## 1. The world-internal starting point

**The world is what it is. There is no outside or observer from nowhere.** This is the organizing methodological rule. A state variable models part of the world; a record models something stored within it; an instrument and an evaluator are further interacting parts. A mathematical quantifier over candidate states does not posit a physically detached observer. The adequacy of the candidate model remains an empirical question.

We distinguish truth at a state, determination by a record, an effective procedure for computing an answer, and permission to act. The legacy code's `authorized` output is a policy value. It is not a definition of knowledge or truth. Different names for generator and verifier do not establish statistical independence or an accurate observation model.

Let a record map and a task be given by

$$r:X\to O,\qquad f:X\to Y.$$

The compatibility fiber contains the model states agreeing with the actual record. A proposition is determined by the record when it holds throughout that fiber:

$$C_r(x)=\{y:r(y)=r(x)\},\qquad
K_r p(x)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \forall y\in C_r(x),\ p(y).$$

This semantics does not by itself describe an agent's awareness, confidence, algorithm or physical ability to enumerate the fiber. False or omitted world models can make an otherwise valid calculation inapplicable. Observation noise requires an enlarged compatibility relation or a stated probabilistic model.

### 1.1 Constructive self-knowledge

**Theorem 1 (Lean).** Record-relative determination is factive, positively introspective, and includes the record's own value:

$$K_r p(x)\Rightarrow p(x),\qquad
K_r p(x)\Rightarrow K_r(K_r p)(x),\qquad
K_r(r=r(x))(x).$$

Factivity uses the actual state in its own fiber. Positive introspection uses transitivity of record equality. Own-record determination uses the definition of the fiber. `determined_is_true`, `positive_introspection` and `own_record_is_determined` are constructive proofs with no axiom dependencies. A more discriminating record preserves previously determined propositions (`refinement_preserves_determination`). These statements refute any reading of self-reference as a blanket impossibility of self-knowledge.

An anti-announced-bit task is different. No Boolean equals its own negation; an adversary defined to negate the announced answer prevents universal correctness for that protocol. `anti_prediction_not_universal` states exactly this obstruction. It says nothing against remembering one's previous outputs or correctly predicting a restricted transition system. Wolpert's inference-device restrictions require their setup-distinguishability hypotheses \[1\]. Logical induction and reflective-oracle constructions give qualified positive self-reference results under different formal models \[2,3\].

## 2. Recovery depends on the task

**Theorem 2 (Lean).** A decoder recovering the task from the record makes the task constant on every record fiber. Conversely, supplying a representative of each realized record constructs a decoder whenever the task is constant on fibers:

$$\exists d:O\to Y\;\forall x,\ d(r(x))=f(x)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\forall x,y,\ r(x)=r(y)\Rightarrow f(x)=f(y).$$

The reverse direction explicitly assumes a representative function whose record agrees with each realized record. Its values on unrealized records are immaterial. This avoids silently invoking a choice principle or an algorithm for arbitrary equivalence classes. `recoverable_iff_fiber_constant` proves the equivalence at that type. A witnessed collision with different task values excludes every exact decoder. Deterministic postprocessing cannot separate equal records. Finite records can nevertheless be sufficient for a finite task, and many states may share a record without losing any answer that task requests.

For finite scalar tasks, the optimal uniform error is half the largest within-fiber diameter. The midpoint of each fiber's extrema attains it. The exact code reports both the midpoint and an extremal collision witness; the companion's `attained_fiber_minimax` proves the real-valued certificate principle. The two values 112 and 143 under one record force error at least 31/2; midpoint 255/2 attains it. Separating their records reduces the error to zero. This is a restriction on recovery from that record, not a restriction on obtaining an additional observation.

## 3. What a memory transformation is

A memory transformation is a concrete change to stored representation: appending an observation, summarizing a transcript, replacing a table with a smaller one, or updating a model after an action. Its correctness is relative to what the memory must still answer. A transformation can discard distinctions irrelevant to a declared task and preserve every answer to that task. Adding another task or action can make the same discarded distinction relevant.

An experiment declares the model identity, readouts, permitted actions, decoder, updater version, comparison class, error allowance, source bindings and storage budget. A changed task or action family is a changed contract. A checksum binds bytes; it does not establish that those bytes describe the world correctly.

Let an action word apply the declared transitions in order. Two states are future-equivalent when every such word yields the same readout:

$$x\sim_F y\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\forall w\in A^*,\ q(F_w(x))=q(F_w(y)).$$

**Theorem 3 (Lean).** Future equivalence is an equivalence relation, preserves the current readout, is stable under each action, and contains every other stable readout-preserving relation. The proof uses the empty word for current agreement and a prefixed word for stability; induction proves maximality. `stable_relation_refines_future_eq` supplies the last direction. Thus the quotient by this relation is the coarsest exact memory for the specified future tasks, at the level of existence of representations. Computing that quotient requires additional effective or finite structure.

This deterministic construction is related to minimal predictive states in computational mechanics and predictive state representations \[4,5\]. Those literatures have their own stochastic, stationarity and almost-sure qualifications. Information bottleneck objectives instead trade information retention against a chosen relevance distribution \[6\]; they do not automatically preserve every future action-conditioned task.

### 3.1 A sufficient implementation contract

For a memory encoder, decoder and updater, the two commuting conditions are

$$d(m(x))=q(x),\qquad m(F_a(x))=U_a(m(x)).$$

**Theorem 4 (Lean).** These conditions imply agreement of every future readout whenever the initial memories agree (`implemented_memory_preserves_future`). They also imply that updating encoded memory along any finite word gives the encoding of the corresponding world state (`run_updates_commute`). This second statement establishes correspondence of actual updates, not only indistinguishability of equal memories.

The executable witness uses a pair of Boolean values, an encoder storing them in reversed order, a decoder reading the second stored component, and identity/swap actions. `pair_memory_commutes` proves the updater equation; `pair_memory_preserves_all_words` proves exact readout preservation after any finite word. Remembering only the first world bit fails after swap: two initially identical records then expose different second bits. The Python example follows the same finite definitions; finite execution checks accompany the Lean proofs, without asserting universal Python-to-Lean refinement.

### 3.2 Capacity and constructive refinement

If a finite system has a given number of future-equivalence classes, any exact memory with the stated updater must have at least that many distinguishable states. The companion proves this by injectivity on representatives (`future_classes_require_memory`). Fixed-length binary storage then needs enough bit strings; variable-length expected coding requires a separate probability and coding model.

The reference algorithm refines a finite partition until every action preserves every class. Independently searching pairs of states produces distinguishing action words. For four states with readout `(0,0,1,1)`, identity alone yields two classes; adding the permutation `(0,2,1,3)` yields four. One bit suffices for the first contract and two for the second. This is a constructive reason to enlarge memory, rather than to conclude that remembering is impossible. Exhaustive four-state checks compare partition refinement against pair-state search on 65,536 state-pair cases.

## 4. Enforced updates, recall and safety

`MemoryImplementation.lean` defines a finite archive of record/answer pairs as compatible when equal records have equal answers. Its executable `appendChecked` returns the concatenated archive only if the entire result is compatible. `accepted_append_compatible` proves that a successful result satisfies the condition; `accepted_append_retains` proves that every old entry remains. A conflicting append is refused, leaving the caller's immutable old value available.

The executable `recallChecked` requires a matching entry and agreement of all stored entries with that record. `accepted_recall_sound` proves both coverage and agreement for every returned answer. A changed archive containing conflicting answers refuses a formerly acceptable recall. These checks concern the complete declared archive, not unstored states. The exact-rational `checked_extension` additionally supports a nonnegative scalar error allowance and independently replays its complete enlarged archive.

**Theorem 5 (Lean, enforced transition invariant).** Given a decidable invariant, a guarded transition executes a proposed next state only when it satisfies that invariant; otherwise it retains the current state. Starting from an invariant-satisfying state, every finite guarded execution preserves the invariant. `gated_run_preserves` proves this for arbitrary types. The concrete pair-state gate rejects an overwrite to `(true,true)`, with `concrete_gate_preserves_every_word` proving the declared invariant for every finite sequence of overwrite requests.

The guard is part of the Lean transition function, so the theorem applies to enforced transitions in that implementation. It does not infer a physical safety predicate from a provenance label. A runtime bypass, unchecked recall entry point, incomplete archive, inaccurate predicate or unsafe initial state falls outside its premise. The included Python functions are a finite reference experiment. This abstract theorem alone does not establish persistence, agent-request injection or actual model use; section 8 supplies separate finite evidence for those implementation questions.

## 5. What the earlier finite RSI theorem actually bounds

The original `RsiImpossible.lean` and `RelationalRsiNoGo.lean` retain their definitions and theorem statements. A duplicate-free list contained in a carrier of size N has length at most N. `OmegaRelationalRsi.rsi_impossible` rules out an infinite strictly improving trajectory whose transitive, irreflexive dominance is invariant under report equality in both arguments and whose report image lies in that fixed finite carrier. It derives distinct reports from those hypotheses using `lemma1c_goodhart_metric_collapse_on_collision`, then applies the finite-list bound. The underlying state space can be infinite. Neither a particular self-modification mechanism nor statistical independence, primitive difference or a sheaf premise occurs in this theorem's signature.

N reports allow at most N−1 report-grounded strict transitions. An arbitrary sequence of N+1 observations repeats by transition index N; this says nothing about the magnitude of a jump or elapsed time. Transcripts, verifier state, timestamps and rolling context must be included in the declared report interface whenever dominance uses them.

Structural `RelationallyAligned` transformations and the numeric-rank `RelationalAlignment` predicate are different definitions, not equivalent encodings. The latter explicitly uses an internal score below N. Relabeling scores can change that policy. The compiled `ReviewCountermodels` exhibit a structurally aligned successor and an infinite increasing trajectory with one constant report; the dominance is not report-grounded. This is a scope control consistent with the finite-report theorem.

`RelationalRSI.rsi_decouples_within_carrier_bound` proves a horizon for its defined increasing internal counter and bounded carrier alignment. Licensed lifts consume a declared finite budget. Coupled-carrier results use a fixed finite product carrier. The overlap and higher-cochain theorems concern declared compatibility equations; failure to glue those data is a different obstruction. None of these results makes every self-modifying process halt or proves that all useful improvements cease.

`PrefixBounds.spending_prefix_invariant` bounds cumulative natural-valued charges plus remaining budget by initial budget, assuming the budget inequality only at indices below a declared finite horizon L. `refinement_prefix_bound` and `lift_prefix_bound` give the corresponding finite-prefix bounds. `interleaved_lift_event_bound` counts actual charged lift events and permits uncharged intervening ticks. The older total strictly decreasing sequences are impossible infinite-descent hypotheses, not certificates of existing finite executions. The retained asynchronous and rewiring corollaries are cardinality statements on fixed report products; they do not formalize buffers, evolving incidence or terminal-reason distributions.

A finite alphabet does not bound the number of complete histories. A binary alphabet has 127 words of length at most six, and arbitrarily many finite words without a length cap. A resource bound must therefore cover total stored configurations, duration, precision, history length or an explicitly fixed quotient. Finite physical dimension alone supplies no unlimited-repetition bound. Repeated measurements, expanding memory and new interventions change the model. Conversely, computation can improve an algorithm even while its evidence is unchanged; observational identifiability and computational difficulty are separate limits.

The earlier policy, quantum and campaign discussion is retained verbatim under `docs/pre_integration_*` as historical source. Its extrapolations are superseded by the explicit scopes here. Evaluator selection does not alone imply zero mutual information; self-report is not automatically a destructive quantum pinching; Bell/contextuality does not establish that reality has no facts. The companion's contextuality results concern specified jointly representable distributions. Quantum statistical comparison requires completely positive and, where used, ancillary-system conditions \[7\]. No new quantum recovery theorem is claimed here.

## 6. Learning and intelligence as resource-dependent capabilities

A useful capability description specifies the environment family, task, observations, actions, working and persistent memory, computation, and accepted error. Lower bounds then identify an intervention that can remove the obstruction: acquire a new observation, retain a distinction, change an action family, take another data pass, or improve computation. These are different changes, with different costs.

The finite query experiment demonstrates the distinction. Three binary queries on four worlds admit a worst-case adaptive depth of two, while every fixed identifying panel requires all three. The companion proves the bounded binary-query recurrence equivalent to existence of a valid plan within that depth and proves error preservation at checked leaves. The finite Python solver returns a plan or a witnessed unresolved search outcome and independently checks optimality. Destructive measurements, forbidden actions and elapsed physical time require additional models.

Fano bounds require a distribution and information assumptions \[8\]. Continual-learning memory lower bounds apply to specified classes, sample regimes and pass constraints; extra passes can change them \[9,10\]. Universal-intelligence quantities depend on their environment coding and prior \[11,12\]. Gödel machines make conditional claims relative to a coded objective and proof system \[13\]. None supplies an assumption-free ceiling on all intelligence or learning.

Statistical improvement claims also need valid evidence after adaptive selection. Confidence sequences and adaptive-data-analysis results address specified sampling and stopping models \[14,15\]. The inspected September 2026 revision of Statistical Gödel Machines accounts for every opened confirmation, including failed ones, with conditionally valid tests and predictable summable error allocations \[16\]. Non-improvement under an objective is different from unsafe behavior. These statistical theorems are imported literature, not Lean results in this package.

## 8. Persistent memory in the live agent

The persistent experiment binds a declared task, model, action set, exact key/value encoding and update rule. Its categorical residual is zero exactly when all recorded observations sharing a key agree. Every recall and proposed update rechecks that residual, source hashes, exact source membership and the contract. Accepted facts enter the repository agent's user context before prompt binding. Rejected updates retain the previous archive; failed recalls prevent dispatch. SQLite transactions and a persisted database carry observations between processes.

The captured exact-use trial used four separate processes: an uninformed baseline returned UNKNOWN, the agent extracted a supplied synthetic observation, and two later processes used the stored value with different requested suffixes. An earlier arithmetic trial failed after correct recall. Thus persistent access and correct downstream reasoning remain different properties. Both outcomes and raw replies are retained, with independent offline replay of prompt bindings and source pins. This is bounded evidence from one model, not a general learning or intelligence benchmark. The connection is to the repository agent facade; it does not modify Codex's global context or model parameters.

The eight Lean statements in `LiveMemory` prove source-supported, consistent updates and recalls, preservation of old entries, refusal behavior and recall after an explicitly exact reload. SQLite crash recovery and the live model's use of context are tested operationally, not proved by these abstract statements. Source adequacy remains a premise. Implementation, commands and full limitations are in `docs/live_memory.md`; captured-trial replay uses `tools/replay_live_memory_evidence.py`.

The preceding numerical verification recorded all 712 quantum-library tests passing in release mode, with no failures, ignored cases or filtered cases. This includes the three prior numerical failures, all ten previously unfinished MUB tests, and three new admission-boundary regressions. The rebuilt native budget host passes all 24 gateway-and-memory tests, including both previously blocked cases; five quantum integration binaries pass 57 tests.
