WHAT THE CENTRAL THEOREM ESTABLISHES

Let r : X → R have a finite image of size q, and let strict dominance be irreflexive, transitive, and invariant under equality of reports in both arguments. If x_0 ≺ x_1 ≺ ... ≺ x_k, then all k+1 reports differ: a repeated report, together with transitivity and report invariance, would give self-dominance. Consequently k+1 ≤ q.

This is the substantive content of OmegaRelationalRsi.rsi_impossible. X itself can be infinite. The proof applies to arbitrary sequences; it does not need a special self-modification mechanism. The independent-verifier rule, primitive-difference data, pre-genesis grammar, and sheaf data are not premises of that declaration.

The useful interpretation is an observer-relative limit on certifiable strict improvement using a fixed, finite, report-sufficient interface. The number of distinct certifiable levels is bounded. The size of each performance jump, elapsed time, and physical evolution after a collision remain unspecified.

Keep this theorem. Put its actual hypotheses before the interpretation.

R01 — P1 — The repair solver issues false exact optimality certificates.

Location: code/exact_dual_witness_solver.py:122–140.
https://github.com/dbccpage/the_symbol_game/blob/e15a9d4ac6298524ce370cae679314aa0fef7585/omega_engine_atlas/coda_atlas/F004_papers/012_rsi/000_relational_reasoning_and_rsi_impossibility/code/exact_dual_witness_solver.py#L122-L140

When Phi_1 is zero, the solver returns the input as the repaired cochain, a zero primal potential, zero residual/cost, and is_certified=True. Even on one filled triangle, take a vertex potential (0,1,2). Its exact edge coboundary is (1,2,1), in edge order (01,02,12). The returned zero potential reconstructs (0,0,0), so the purported primal certificate does not reconstruct the purported repair.

The deeper failure is the identification ker(d^k) = im(d^{k-1}). On the supported six-triangle annulus in reproduce_findings.py, every face coboundary is zero and the outer-loop period is 3. The solver reports optimal repair cost 0. An independently checked rational dual witness has D^T w=0, ||w||∞=1, and <w,delta>=9; the zero potential attains L1 cost 9. The exact optimum is therefore 9.

This is an incorrect mathematical result marked certified, not floating-point uncertainty.

Repair: remove the closedness shortcut unless an actual potential is recovered and checked. Before issuing a certificate, independently check delta_rep=D eta, residual=delta-delta_rep, primal cost, dual feasibility, and exact objective equality. A closedness test cannot replace an exactness test on a general complex. Add the nonzero exact triangle and the closed non-exact annulus as regression cases.

https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.07918
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1705.07918
