### 2. Formal Specification of the Quantum Relational Universe
Define the **Quantum Relational Universe** $\mathcal{U}_Q = (\mathcal{H}, \mathcal{S}_Q, \Pi, C_{\mathrm{rep}}, \operatorname{report})$:
- Let $\mathcal{H} \cong \mathbb{C}^d$ be a finite-dimensional Hilbert space ($d \ge 2$), or $K^d$ over a cyclotomic number field $K = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_n)$.
- Let $\mathcal{S}_Q = \{\rho \in \mathcal{L}(\mathcal{H}) \mid \rho = \rho^\dagger, \rho \ge 0, \mathrm{Tr}(\rho) = 1\}$ be the convex set of density operators.
- Let $\Pi = \{E_1, \dots, E_M\}$ be a finite Positive Operator-Valued Measure (POVM) satisfying $E_m \ge 0$ for all $m \in \{1, \dots, M\}$ and $\sum_{m=1}^M E_m = I$. The discrete outcome alphabet is $Y = \{1, \dots, M\}$.
- Following Definition 2.1 of Monograph 012-000, any intersubjective communicative channel factorizes through a boundary alphabet:
  $$\tau = \iota \circ \kappa \circ \pi : \mathcal{S}_Q \to C_{\mathrm{rep}}$$
  where $\pi(\rho) = (\mathrm{Tr}(\rho E_1), \dots, \mathrm{Tr}(\rho E_M))$ represents the probability profile across the finite measurement panel, and $\operatorname{report}(\rho) \in C_{\mathrm{rep}}$ is an intersubjective report derived from $\pi(\rho)$ (such as discretized outcome histograms or detector click profiles of capacity $N = |C_{\mathrm{rep}}| \le M$).

### 3. Accessible Information & Holevo Horizon Finitude
- Formulate the relation between Holevo's accessible information bound ($\chi \le \log_2 d$) and the effective capacity of the intersubjective report carrier $C_{\mathrm{rep}}$.
- State and prove **Lemma Q.1 (POVM Report Carrier Finitude):**
  Even though $\mathcal{S}_Q$ contains uncountably many states, any intersubjective observation factorized through a finite POVM panel $\Pi$ of $M$ outcomes produces an effective report space $C_{\mathrm{rep}}$ of finite cardinality bounded by $M$ (or $M^k$ for $k$ repeated trials).

### 4. Strict Report-Grounded Dominance on Quantum States
- Define a strict dominance relation $(\succ) : \mathcal{S}_Q \to \mathcal{S}_Q \to \mathsf{Prop}$ satisfying:
  - Irreflexivity: $\forall \rho \in \mathcal{S}_Q, \neg(\rho \succ \rho)$.
  - Transitivity: $\forall \rho, \sigma, \tau \in \mathcal{S}_Q, \rho \succ \sigma \land \sigma \succ \tau \implies \rho \succ \tau$.
- Define **Report-Grounded Dominance:**
  $$\forall \rho_1, \rho_2, \sigma_1, \sigma_2 \in \mathcal{S}_Q,\quad \operatorname{report}(\rho_1) = \operatorname{report}(\rho_2) \land \operatorname{report}(\sigma_1) = \operatorname{report}(\sigma_2) \land (\rho_1 \succ \sigma_1) \implies (\rho_2 \succ \sigma_2)$$

### 5. Proof of Theorem Q.1 (Quantum Bounded RSI Horizon Theorem — Report Collision)
- Formulate a recursive self-modifying quantum trajectory $(\rho_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ where $\rho_{k+1} = \mathcal{E}_k(\rho_k)$, with each $\mathcal{E}_k$ being an internal completely positive trace-preserving (CPTP) transition map, claiming strict report-grounded improvement $\rho_{k+1} \succ \rho_k$.
- Prove that by step $k^* \le |C_{\mathrm{rep}}| + 1 \le M + 1$:
  - A report collision $\operatorname{report}(\rho_i) = \operatorname{report}(\rho_j)$ must occur for some $i < j$.
  - By transitivity, $\rho_j \succ \rho_i$.
  - Combined with report identity $\operatorname{report}(\rho_j) = \operatorname{report}(\rho_i)$, report-grounded dominance forces $\rho_i \succ \rho_i$, which contradicts irreflexivity.
  - Conclude that no infinite strictly improving trajectory can exist, and termination terminates via `REPORT_COLLISION` at step $k^* \le M + 1$.
- Address Goodhart proxy decoupling: If an agent adopts an internal proxy scalar $\mu(\rho_k)$, show that $\mu(\rho_k) \ge k$ decouples from the finite report carrier bound at $k \ge M$.

### 6. Stabilizer Subsector Orbit Finitude
- Restrict $\mathcal{S}_Q$ to $n$-qubit stabilizer states under Clifford group operations (Target 12 and Target 18 in Paper 006).
- Formulate the Gottesman-Knill theorem showing that stabilizer orbits are finite discrete relational structures with exact cardinality:
  $$|Stab_n| = 2^n \prod_{j=1}^n (2^{2j-1} + 1)$$
- Prove Corollary Q.1.1: For any recursive self-modifying stabilizer agent, termination via `REPORT_COLLISION` occurs at step $k^* \le |Stab_n| + 1$.
- Cite the Eastin-Knill theorem: Explain why transversal gates alone cannot supply non-Clifford transitions, demonstrating that escaping the stabilizer orbit requires external gate injection (which consumes an external lift budget).
